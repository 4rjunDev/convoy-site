Common questions

Why can't my friends see me? Everyone must be in the same group and cruise, with location allowed for Convoy. Your car appears once your phone has a location fix.

What is a thru-point? A point the route must pass through without stopping. Use a stop for places the convoy parks, such as a fuel or lunch break.

Does Convoy track me when it's closed? No. Location is used while the app is open and, so your phone can sit locked in a cradle, during a live convoy or route. It stops when the convoy or route ends.

How do I delete my account? In Convoy, open My profile and tap Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is removed.

Driving safely

Set up the route before you drive, and let a passenger handle the phone. Follow road signs and the law over any instruction in the app.
