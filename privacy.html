Convoy Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Convoy is an app for driving together: friends join a cruise, follow a shared route, see each other on the map, report hazards and send short voice messages. This policy explains what Convoy collects to do that, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Convoy has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password, used to sign in. Passwords are stored hashed by our backend provider and are never visible to us.
  • Profile: the display name, username, vehicle name and vehicle icon you choose. Other Convoy users can see these.
  • Precise location: collected only while Convoy is open, and only after you allow it. When you are in a live convoy, your latest position and whether you are driving or stopped are shared with the other members of that convoy. We keep your most recent position, not a history of where you have been.
  • Voice messages: recorded only while you use the Talk button, and sent to the convoy members you choose. Messages are deleted about 10 minutes after they are sent.
  • Hazard reports: the type, location, optional note and your display name. Any Convoy driver nearby can see a report. Reports are deleted about 45 minutes after they are made.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store your device's push token so we can tell you when a cruise route is released. It is removed when you sign out.
  • Groups and cruises: the groups you create or join, the cruises you plan, and their routes and members.

Convoy does not access your contacts, photos or advertising identifier, and does not use your location in the background.

How we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your convoy on the map, planning and guiding routes, delivering hazard reports and voice messages, and letting you manage groups and cruises.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts Convoy's database and sign-in. Your data is stored on Supabase's servers, which may be outside your country. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Apple Maps draws the map and calculates routes. To do that, your device sends Apple the map area you are viewing and the points of your route. Apple handles this under Apple Maps & Privacy.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Convoy under My profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, location, voice messages, group memberships and the cruises you organised. Groups you run are handed to another member, or removed if you were the only member. Hazard reports you made disappear when they expire.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for deletion.

Children

Convoy is for drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
